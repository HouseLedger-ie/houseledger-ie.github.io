Privacy and community submissions

Pre-launch draft. The website owner must complete this notice before inviting public submissions.

HouseLedger collects county-level house details, costs, and optional homeowner notes that contributors choose to provide. Approved details are published for visitors to browse and compare. Unapproved submissions can be seen by authorised moderators.

Spreadsheet files are processed in your browser to prefill the form; the original files are not uploaded to the site. Only the information you review and submit is sent to the database.

Do not include names, addresses, telephone numbers, bank details, or other identifying information in free-text fields. Anonymous publication does not guarantee that a distinctive house cannot be recognised.

The site owner needs to specify the lawful basis for processing, controller identity, contact address, hosting region, retention policy, rights-request process and deletion procedure before launch.

Approval means a moderator has reviewed the submission; it does not mean the expenditure has been independently verified. Submitted amounts are not financial advice or official estimates.
