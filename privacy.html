HouseLedger privacy notice

Updated 12 November 2026

Who is responsible?

HouseLedger is an independent community project operated by Darragh Pilkington as an individual. Darragh Pilkington is the data controller for personal information collected through HouseLedger. For privacy questions, access, correction, objection or removal requests, use the private contact form.

What we collect

Voluntary building submissions can include county, year, floor area, property characteristics, costs, cost breakdowns, free-text descriptions, declared source category (homeowner, builder or public online report), cost basis (total project, builder contract or unspecified) and whether an amount is approximate. For online reports, the original public page URL is collected for private moderator review. Such a link may identify the original author, even though it is not published on HouseLedger. Private enquiries include the email address, request type, optional listing reference and message supplied. Supabase handles administrator authentication. Website, database and anti-abuse providers may process technical data such as IP addresses and request metadata.

Do not submit names, exact home addresses, contact details, account details, invoices or confidential information in public listing fields. Original CSV/Excel files selected for import are read in your browser and are not themselves uploaded by the importer; only the information you review and submit is sent to HouseLedger.

Purposes and legal bases

We review and publish suitable contributed or publicly reported building-cost figures to help people understand and compare Irish self-build costs. Where submissions may constitute personal data, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating a useful, moderated, community-contributed cost comparison service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), subject to assessing necessity and the impact on individuals. We also rely on legitimate interests to prevent abuse and handle ordinary enquiries. Where we handle requests under data-protection law, processing necessary to meet those duties relies on legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us. Submitting a build is voluntary.

What is published?

Only moderator-approved listings are publicly displayed. They show county-level location, source category, cost basis and whether an amount is approximate, rather than exact addresses or source URLs. Source links are available only to authorised administrators. A distinctive property or an unusual combination of facts might still be identifiable, including by searching the original public report. Third-party reports require a separate privacy and reuse assessment before approval. Where a historic public report lacks a completion year or other project details, those fields are shown as not stated rather than guessed. Please only share information you are entitled to disclose. HouseLedger does not independently verify reported amounts, contributor identities, or provide financial advice. Builder contract values and homeowners' all-in project costs are not directly comparable.

Retention

  • Pending house submissions, including any private online source reference: deleted after 90 days if not approved or rejected.
  • Rejected house submissions, including associated private source references: deleted 30 days after rejection.
  • Approved listings and related private moderator source references: kept while relevant to the comparison service, subject to review, correction and removal requests.
  • Private enquiries marked resolved: deleted 12 months after resolution, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.
  • Unresolved enquiries: retained while needed to deal with the request; reviewed rather than automatically deleted under the resolved-enquiry rule.

Scheduled deletion applies to HouseLedger database records; service-provider logs and backups may have separate retention arrangements. Replies sent by email may also remain in the operator's email account until deleted under an appropriate retention process.

Service providers and international processing

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for database storage and administrator authentication, and Cloudflare Turnstile for automated-abuse protection. HouseLedger's primary Supabase database is hosted in Paris, France. Other processing, including service logs, backups, security checks, email correspondence and subprocessors, may take place in other locations, including outside the European Economic Area. EU database hosting alone does not mean all processing remains in the EU. We are reviewing the applicable provider transfer arrangements and safeguards.

Provider information: GitHub privacy statement, Supabase privacy policy, Cloudflare privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to, correction or erasure of personal data, restriction of processing, or object to processing; data portability applies where the GDPR conditions are met. Send requests through our private contact form. We may ask for reasonable evidence linking you to a listing before making changes. We normally respond to GDPR rights requests within one month, subject to applicable legal exceptions and extensions. You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Contact and changes

The private contact form sends messages to an administrator-only inbox. If you believe a public-source listing concerns your property, you can ask us to review, correct or remove it. We use the email address you provide to reply to your enquiry, including privacy requests. Replies may come from the operator's personal email account, so that sender address and name may be visible to you. Your email address and message are not published on HouseLedger. The form does not currently issue automatic email confirmations. We may revise this notice as the service develops and will update the date above.

Pilot notice: HouseLedger is a small pilot. We are reviewing service-provider arrangements and safeguards. Please avoid including unnecessary identifying or confidential information in submissions or enquiries.
