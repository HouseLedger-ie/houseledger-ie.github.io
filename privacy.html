Privacy & community submissions

Pre-launch notice — not yet final. The site owner still needs to confirm the legal controller identity, lawful basis and retention periods before inviting public submissions. Please do not submit identifiable information while this is incomplete.

What HouseLedger does

HouseLedger is a community-maintained database of self-reported house-building costs in Ireland. Homeowners can provide county-level location, build area, year, total price, optional breakdown figures, construction details and personal observations. The site owner reviews contributions before making them visible publicly.

Information handled

  • Submitted house details and cost information, including text entered into the notes, inclusions and exclusions fields.
  • Site administrator authentication is handled by Supabase; administrators can see unapproved submissions.
  • Excel and CSV files selected for import are read in your browser to populate the form. The original files are not uploaded to HouseLedger by the importer; only information you review and submit is sent to the backend.
  • Standard hosting and database service logs may contain technical information such as IP addresses, depending on the providers' logging policies.

Publication and sharing

Only moderator-approved build entries are displayed publicly. Public visitors can search and compare approved data. Entries show a county, not an exact street address. HouseLedger does not independently verify amounts. The creator of a distinctive home could nevertheless be identifiable from the submitted details. Please avoid names, exact addresses, contact information, account details, invoices or any confidential information in the text fields.

Hosting

The website is currently deployed using GitHub Pages. The HouseLedger database is hosted on Supabase in an EU region (Paris, France). These services have their own processing and infrastructure arrangements. No original cost spreadsheets are stored in the database through the current import feature.

Changes and removals

The site administrator can correct, reject and permanently delete submissions. A private contact form is available for correction or removal requests. The administrator may need to ask for evidence of your connection to a listing before modifying or removing it.

Submit a private correction, removal or privacy enquiry. Your details go directly to HouseLedger’s private administrator inbox and are not visible to other website visitors. We cannot currently send automatic email confirmations.

Still to be finalised

The website operator must identify the data controller, establish and describe the applicable GDPR lawful basis for processing (including publication), define retention periods for pending/rejected/published entries, give the operator’s actual identity and an appropriate explanation of data-subject rights and complaints to the Irish Data Protection Commission. This draft is not a substitute for a reviewed privacy notice.

HouseLedger data is self-reported; approval is not independent cost verification or financial advice.
